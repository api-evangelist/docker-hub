We’ve all been there: you need to generate a few images for a project, you fire up an AI image service, and suddenly you’re wondering what happens to your prompts, how many credits you have left, or why that “safe content” filter rejected your perfectly reasonable request for a dragon wearing a business suit. What if you could skip all of that and run the whole thing on your own machine, with a slick chat UI on top? That’s exactly what Docker Model Runner now makes possible.
