We’re excited to announce the general availability of Custom Catalogs and Profiles for managing Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. These two complementary capabilities fundamentally change how teams package, distribute, and manage AI tooling. Custom MCP Catalogs let organizations curate and distribute approved collections of MCP servers.
