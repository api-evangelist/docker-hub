AI governance is the system of frameworks, policies, and controls that direct how an organization builds, deploys, and oversees artificial intelligence. According to Docker's State of Agentic AI report, 60% of organizations already have AI agents in production, yet 40% cite security and compliance as the number-one barrier to scaling them further. As AI takes on higher-stakes decisions and agents operate with greater autonomy, organizations without clear guardrails face mounting exposure to regulatory penalties, security vulnerabilities, and reputational damage.
